How to read this chart

Each line is one session's constant-maturity curve: the settlement curve interpolated to fixed maturities of 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150 calendar days.

Fixed maturities are what make the sessions comparable. Real contracts lose a day of life every session, so raw contract curves shift sideways underneath you and shape change gets confused with the passage of time.

The buttons are independent, so any combination of prior sessions can sit on the chart at once. The scale is computed from all five and does not move.
