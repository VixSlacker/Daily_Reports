Fixed maturities of 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150 calendar days, interpolated from
standardised monthly VX futures settlements. Basis is VIX minus CM30, in volatility
points: spot measured against the front of the futures curve. Usually negative;
positive when spot jumps above the front, as in a spike. Segments are the longer
maturity minus the shorter, in volatility points, so contango is positive and a
larger number is a steeper stretch.
